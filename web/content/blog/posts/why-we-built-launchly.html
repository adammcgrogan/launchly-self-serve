Plenty of good local businesses don't have a website. Not because they don't see the value in one, and not because they can't afford it — but because the usual path from "I need a website" to "I have a website" is a lot to ask of someone who's already running a business day to day.

Brief a designer, wait for mockups, go back and forth on revisions, figure out hosting, wait for someone to actually publish it. Weeks pass. For a business owner who's already stretched thin, that's often enough friction that the website just never happens.

What we changed

Launchly is built to remove that friction entirely. Sign up with an email and password — no card required. Pick a design. Add your own content. Your site is live at your own subdomain the same day, with no approval queue and no one else deciding when it goes live.

If a customer gets in touch through your contact form, that lead is saved and emailed to you automatically. If you want a different look later, you can switch designs any time without losing your content. If you want to upgrade, that's a button in your dashboard, not an email chasing an invoice.

Who it's for

Electricians, cafes, gyms, salons, shops — local businesses that need a real site and don't have time to brief a designer. That's the whole focus. Not a general-purpose website builder trying to be everything to everyone — a tool built specifically for the businesses that actually make up a local high street.
